About the CSE Department

The Department of Computer Science & Engineering (CSE) at Trident Academy of Technology is committed to delivering quality education with a strong balance of theory and practical learning. The department focuses on building problem-solving skills, programming expertise, and industry-ready professionals.

Students gain exposure to modern technologies including Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Web Development, and Cloud Computing through hands-on projects and real-world applications.

Program Highlights

  • Industry-oriented and updated curriculum
  • Highly experienced and supportive faculty
  • Well-equipped modern computer laboratories
  • Strong focus on coding and logical thinking
  • Regular workshops, hackathons, and internships
