Admission Overview

Trident Academy of Technology offers admissions for Undergraduate (B.Tech), Postgraduate (M.Tech, MBA, MCA) and Diploma programs. Eligibility and entrance criteria vary by course. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

Courses Offered

  • B.Tech – Various Specializations
  • M.Tech – Advanced & Technical Programs
  • MBA – Management Studies
  • MCA – Computer Applications
  • Diploma in Engineering

Eligibility Criteria

For B.Tech: Must have passed 10+2 with Physics, Mathematics and one of Chemistry / Biology / Biotechnology / Technical vocational subject with a minimum percentage (General / Reserved as per institute policy). :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

Lateral Entry (2nd Year): Diploma holders with relevant engineering diploma are eligible for direct admission to 2nd year of B.Tech after OJEE. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

MCA: Bachelor’s in any discipline with Mathematics at 10+2 or graduation level. :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}

MBA: Bachelor’s degree in any discipline with minimum qualifying marks. :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}

M.Tech: B.Tech / M.Sc in relevant field with qualifying marks. :contentReference[oaicite:6]{index=6}

Admission Process

  1. Check eligibility for your chosen program.
  2. Apply via the official admission form (online or on campus).
  3. Participate in counselling and entrance tests as required (JEE Main / OJEE / PGAT).
  4. Submit documents and complete fee payment to confirm your seat. :contentReference[oaicite:7]{index=7}
