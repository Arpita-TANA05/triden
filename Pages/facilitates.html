Campus Facilities

A learning environment designed for innovation, comfort and success.

Advanced Laboratories

Trident Academy of Technology houses well-equipped laboratories for Computer Science, Mechanical, Electrical, Civil and Electronics departments. All labs are regularly updated with modern tools, software and safety standards.

  • Computer & Programming Labs
  • Mechanical & Thermal Labs
  • Electrical & Power Systems Labs
  • Civil Engineering Labs

Central Library

The central library provides a rich collection of textbooks, reference books, journals, research papers and digital resources. Students have access to both physical and online learning materials.

  • Thousands of academic books
  • E-journals & digital library
  • Peaceful reading environment

Hostel & Accommodation

Separate hostels for boys and girls with 24×7 security, hygienic food, study rooms and recreational facilities ensure a comfortable campus life.

  • Wi-Fi enabled hostels
  • Mess & dining facilities
  • Medical & security support

Sports & Recreation

Physical fitness and teamwork are encouraged through various indoor and outdoor sports facilities, promoting a healthy campus lifestyle.

  • Cricket, Football & Volleyball grounds
  • Indoor games facilities
  • Annual sports events
