About the Electrical Engineering Department

The Department of Electrical Engineering at Trident Academy of Technology offers a comprehensive curriculum covering power systems, electrical machines, control systems, and renewable energy technologies.

Students receive strong practical exposure through modern laboratories and industry-oriented training to meet real-world engineering challenges.
