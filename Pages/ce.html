About the Department

The Civil Engineering department offers strong theoretical knowledge combined with practical exposure in construction technology, structural analysis, geotechnical engineering and environmental studies. Students are trained to solve real-world infrastructure challenges.

Department Highlights

Structural Engineering

Design and analysis of buildings, bridges and structures.

Transportation Engineering

Planning and construction of roads and highways.

Environmental Engineering

Water resources, waste management and sustainability.

Qualified Faculty

Experienced faculty with academic and industry expertise.

Laboratory Facilities

Concrete Technology Lab

Testing of cement, concrete and construction materials.

Geotechnical Engineering Lab

Soil testing and foundation engineering practices.

Surveying Lab

Field training using modern surveying instruments.
