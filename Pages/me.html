About the Mechanical Engineering Department

The Department of Mechanical Engineering at Trident Academy of Technology provides a strong foundation in core engineering concepts along with practical exposure through modern laboratories and workshops.

The curriculum is designed to meet industry requirements and focuses on design, manufacturing, automation, and emerging technologies.
