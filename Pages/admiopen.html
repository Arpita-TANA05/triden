Admission Overview

Trident Academy of Technology offers admissions for Undergraduate (B.Tech), Postgraduate (M.Tech, MBA, MCA) and Diploma programs. Eligibility and entrance criteria vary by course.

  • B.Tech – Various Specializations
  • M.Tech – Advanced & Technical Programs
  • MBA – Management Studies
  • MCA – Computer Applications
  • Diploma in Engineering

Eligibility Criteria

B.Tech: Passed 10+2 with Physics, Mathematics and one of Chemistry / Biology / Biotechnology / Technical Vocational subjects.

Lateral Entry: Diploma holders eligible for direct admission to 2nd year B.Tech through OJEE.

MCA: Bachelor’s degree with Mathematics.

MBA: Bachelor’s degree in any discipline.

M.Tech: B.Tech / M.Sc in relevant discipline.

Courses Offered

  • B.Tech
  • M.Tech
  • MBA
  • MCA
  • Diploma

Admission Process

  1. Check Eligibility
  2. Apply Online / Offline
  3. Entrance Exam (JEE / OJEE)
  4. Counselling
